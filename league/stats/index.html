League Stats

Superlative points (SP). 01 rounds earn 1 point for 100–119, 2 for 120–139, 3 for 140–159, 4 for 160–179 and 5 for a 180. Cricket rounds earn 1–5 points for 5–9 marks, or 1, 2, 3 or 5 points for 3, 4, 5 or 6 bulls, whichever is higher. Only marks that close or score a number count. SP / Leg divides by every 01 and cricket leg played.
